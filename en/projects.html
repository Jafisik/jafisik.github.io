My projects

GLYPHORA (C#)

GLYPHORA is a typeracer rougelite game inspired by Balatro. The player upgrades their keyboard and by writing the text on the screen they defeat enemies while getting stronger. The game is now available on the Steam platform.

Portfolio for Luvxum (JS)

A portfolio website for Natálie Svozilová. It showcases her paintings and photos.

JETI Studio (C++)

JETI Studio is a cross-platform program for the visualization and analysis of telemetry data received from sensors created by JETI Model. I've created the graph window and debugged other features inside of the application.

Car game (C#)

In this game (made in Unity) you play as a car (controlled by WASD) and try to beat other cars controlled by an ai. I've made this game for 2 of my university lectures. The first lecture was on game development and the other one on ai learning.

Minesweeper bot (Python)

This application solves the game Minesweeper by pressing the most optimal squares. If there are no moves with a 100% chance, then it chooses the square with the least bomb chance.

CubeTPS Demo (C#)

This is my first go on a unity game. It's a third person shooter, where you collect coins to win. There are also breakable red blocks and purple blocks that restrict you from jumping.
